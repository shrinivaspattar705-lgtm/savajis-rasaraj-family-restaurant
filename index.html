About

Tradition, Family, and Premium Savaji Delights

Savaji's Rasaraj is more than a restaurant — it is a celebration of North Karnataka's bold Savaji culture.

For over 21 years, families and food lovers have gathered here to enjoy authentic home-style mutton delicacies crafted with passion and unforgettable flavor.
